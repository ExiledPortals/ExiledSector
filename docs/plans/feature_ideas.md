# Feature ideas

Early ideas for possible features, kept here so they aren't forgotten. None is designed or built. When one gets a real design, move it to its own plan in `docs/plans/` and link it here. Delete it from this list once it's built.

New skill effects and nodes go in `skill_effect_ideas.md` instead.

## Skill tree IVs

Hidden values rolled once per ship, like a Pokémon's individual values (IVs). A ship found with a rare roll might have more points to spend, or even unique nodes no other ship of its hull can take. Finding and keeping a well-rolled ship becomes a goal of its own.

## Better raids

A large overhaul of ground raids, replacing vanilla's dialog of marines against ground defense strength with an actual fought battle, like ship combat:

- a battle arena;
- deploying soldiers or mechs, with AI to control them;
- terrain that suits where the raid takes place;
- all tied in with skill effects, and combat units that can be rolled with currency items.
